About me

About

I’m a computer science researcher who brings together data, AI, and thoughtful design to build useful technology. My interests span healthcare, economics, and how people interact with intelligent systems.

I work with Python, SQL, Java, and R, and have built projects in data science, machine learning, data analytics, and visualization. I also develop Flask-based applications and websites, combining frontend and backend development with responsive design and UX/UI.

From studying human activity with AI to exploring health data, I enjoy turning complex questions into clear insights and accessible digital experiences.
